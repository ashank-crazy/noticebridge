# NoticeBridge demo

Narrated walkthrough using actual browser captures and the fictional museum-trip sample.

## 00:00:00 — THE PROBLEM

School circulars arrive as PDFs, photos, and class-group messages. The small details are easy to lose. NoticeBridge gives a family one noticeboard for what needs doing.

## 00:00:12 — PIN A NOTICE

Pin a notice from a PDF, English screenshot, or pasted text. File reading happens in the browser. This walkthrough uses the built-in museum trip sample and local English extraction.

## 00:00:25 — TRACE EVERY STEP

The circular becomes a checklist: submit the consent form, pay the fee, and pack for the trip. Every extracted step keeps an exact quote from the source.

## 00:00:36 — CHECK THE DATE

Before creating a reminder, check the date against the highlighted sentence. Confirm it yourself. Missing years and unclear dates stay out of the export until they are reviewed.

## 00:00:47 — THE SCHOOL SENDS AN UPDATE

Now the school sends a revision. The consent deadline moves from the twenty-fourth to the twenty-seventh of October. NoticeBridge crosses out the old date and asks for confirmation again.

## 00:00:58 — CATCH THE OTHER CHANGE

The packing list also adds a school ID card. Both changes are marked, while the unchanged payment stays completed. The current circular remains beside the checklist for checking.

## 00:01:10 — HELP AT HOME

English and Hindi board controls, Hindi sample instructions, and device read-aloud help families use the same plan. Full translation of a family's own notices is available through optional AI processing.

## 00:01:24 — PASS THE PLAN ON

The family checklist puts the revised instructions, dates, completion state, and original quotes into one sheet. Copy it to a family group, save the text, or print it for home.

## 00:01:36 — THE DATE SHEET

The date sheet brings the remaining deadlines together. Blue marks confirmed dates. The yellow mark here is a date that still needs review.

## 00:01:45 — TAKE THE DATE WITH YOU

Only unfinished steps with confirmed dates are exported. In this example, the calendar file contains one all-day reminder for the new consent deadline. It can be imported into a calendar app.

## 00:01:57 — KEEP YOUR BOARD

The board is saved on this device. Download a backup to move circulars, progress, and checked dates to another device. Restoring shows a preview before replacing the current board.

## 00:02:09 — NOTICEBRIDGE

NoticeBridge connects a school circular to the next step at home. Trace the instruction. Catch the revision. Share the plan. The source code and setup are available on GitHub.
